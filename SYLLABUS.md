# CS756 — Project-I

**Semester:** 7 | **Credits:** 3 | **L-T-P:** 0-0-6
**Category:** Project (PW) | **Max Marks:** 100 (Internal only)

## Course Outcomes
On completion of this course, a student will be able to:
1. Apply knowledge from previous semesters to undertake and solve a real-life problem.
2. Illustrate the solution after identifying various objectives of the problem undertaken.
3. Devise an organised action plan along with all team members.
4. Develop a solution using appropriate methodology and available tools.
5. Communicate and demonstrate the work through a structured report and oral presentation.

## Syllabus / Requirement
Students are required to submit a working project demonstrating the acquired computer science and engineering skills.

## Evaluation
Continuous evaluation by the project mentor throughout the semester, based on the progress of the project. Multiple presentations may be scheduled. Evaluation is on the basis of defined rubrics through semester presentations, working projects, project reports and viva voce.
