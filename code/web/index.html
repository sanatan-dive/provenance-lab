How it works

  1. Check provenance

    Look for C2PA content credentials, AudioSeal audio watermarks and text watermarks. If one is present, that is proof, and we stop.

  2. Extract forensic signals

    Perplexity and burstiness for essays, syntax-tree structure for code, the frequency spectrum for images, MFCC and phase for voice.

  3. Encode with a deep model

    BERT for essays, CodeBERT for code, a Vision Transformer plus a frequency CNN for images, and wav2vec 2.0 speech features for voice.

  4. Learn with contrastive loss

    Training pulls human samples together and pushes AI samples away, so the model learns what machine-made content has in common rather than one generator's quirks.

  5. Decide, or say we're unsure

    Above 70% is called AI, below 30% human. Anything between is flagged for a person to review, because a wrong accusation costs more than a missed one.
